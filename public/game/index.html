DRIVER'S BRIEF

SURVIVE THE PACK.

Last car running wins 2 points. First to 6 wins the match. At 55 seconds, the leading surviving car wins the round.

Fall from an exposed edge, lose all 100 HP, or trail the leader by 68 metres for 3 seconds and you're out. Watch the next round and jump back in.

Weapon shapes identify pickups. Empty pads refill over 7 seconds. Watch for incoming-rocket alerts and confirmed-hit messages. Motion can be reduced with the top-bar switch.

DRIFTBuild speed above 36 km/h, then hold Space with gas and steer into the corner. Keep holding Space; steer into the slide to tighten it or countersteer to widen it. Release Space to regain grip. S / Down still brakes.

DRIVEWASD or arrow keys
S / Down: brake, then reverse
Space + steer: hold to drift
F / Enter: weapon
R: recover (−20 HP)

TOUCHHold LEFT / RIGHT to steer.
Hold GAS to accelerate.
Hold DRIFT with GAS and steer to slide. Release DRIFT to regain grip.
FIRE uses your pickup; REV reverses.

GAMEPADLeft stick: steer
RT / A: accelerate
LT / B: reverse
X: drift · RB: fire

MUSICOriginal synthesized C-minor funk at 96 BPM. Bass opens alone; drums, guitar and piano build patiently. Beethoven’s Fifth enters after 80 seconds, then swells every minute. The groove continues across rounds. Toggle MUSIC independently of engine and effects.
